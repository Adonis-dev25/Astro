Emotional Support

Support is not about fixing everything. Often the most helpful thing is to stay close, listen well and make your partner feel they are not alone.

Key Ideas

Be present

Put the phone down. Full attention is a powerful gift.

Validate feelings

"That sounds really hard" works better than "Don't worry".

Ask what they need

Some want advice, others only want a listener. Just ask.

Support their stress

Exams, work, family pressure: share the load where you can.

Do and Avoid

Do

  • Check in during hard weeks
  • Offer practical help
  • Celebrate their wins
  • Be patient with bad days

Avoid

  • Say "you are overreacting"
  • Rush to solve everything
  • Make their pain about you
  • Disappear when things get heavy

Try This

  1. Listen without interrupting.
  2. Reflect back what you heard.
  3. Ask, "How can I help right now?"
  4. Follow up a day or two later.
"Sometimes the best thing you can say is nothing. Just be there."
- Common saying

Common Challenges

When they will not open up

Do not push. Let them know the door is always open.

When you are drained too

You cannot pour from an empty cup. Look after yourself so you can keep supporting.

Serious struggles

If your partner seems depressed or unsafe, encourage them to speak to a counsellor, doctor or trusted adult.
