Get Help and Resources

Everyone needs support sometimes. Asking for help is a sign of strength, and you do not have to face problems alone.

Free and confidential helplines in Zambia

Lifeline/Childline Zambia offers free counselling, open every day, on any network (Airtel, MTN and Zamtel).

116ChildlineFor children and young people: abuse, bullying, online safety, mental health 933Gender-Based Violence LineFor adults: abuse, domestic violence, mental health and relationship problems

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, go to a safe place and contact the police or a trusted adult right away.

Healthy or unhealthy? Know the signs

Healthy signs

  • You can say no and it is respected
  • You both keep your friends and interests
  • Disagreements are talked through calmly
  • You feel safe, valued and free to be yourself
  • Mistakes lead to honest apologies

Warning signs

  • Constant checking of your phone or location
  • Pressure to cut off friends or family
  • Insults, threats, shouting or breaking things
  • Guilt or punishment when you say no
  • Fear of how they will react

How to help a friend

  1. Listen without judging. Let them speak at their own pace.
  2. Believe them and tell them it is not their fault.
  3. Do not pressure them to leave or confront anyone. Ask what they want.
  4. Encourage them to talk to a trusted adult, a counsellor, or one of the helplines above.
  5. Keep checking in. Staying connected matters.
