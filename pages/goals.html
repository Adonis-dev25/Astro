Relationship Goals

Strong couples are not only in love with each other. They are walking in the same direction. Shared goals turn two lives into a team.

Key Ideas

Shared dreams

Talk openly about school, career, family and where you want to live.

Money habits

Agree on how you will save, spend and handle money pressure.

Personal growth

Cheer for each other's individual goals, not only your joint ones.

Fun and rest

Plan dates, trips and downtime. Joy is a goal too.

Do and Avoid

Do

  • Write goals down together
  • Break big goals into small steps
  • Review progress every few months
  • Adjust plans as life changes

Avoid

  • Force your dream on them
  • Compare your relationship to others online
  • Postpone every conversation about the future
  • Let goals replace quality time

Try This

  1. Each person lists three hopes for the next year.
  2. Find where they overlap.
  3. Choose one small action for this month.
  4. Celebrate when you hit it.
"Coming together is a beginning, staying together is progress, and working together is success."
- Henry Ford

Common Challenges

Different timelines

Compromise: you may not move at the same speed, but you can agree on the direction.

Competing priorities

Study, work and family all pull on your time. Be honest about what you can give.

Losing motivation

Return to why you started. Small wins rebuild momentum.
