Communication

Most arguments are not about the thing being argued over. They are about feeling unheard. Good communication is a skill, and like any skill it improves with practice.

Key Ideas

Listen to understand

Let your partner finish. Repeat back what you heard before you respond.

Say how you feel

Start with "I feel..." instead of "You always...". It lowers defences.

Pick the right moment

Serious talks go badly when one of you is tired, hungry or rushed.

Watch body language

Tone, eye contact and posture often say more than the words do.

Do and Avoid

Do

  • Ask open questions
  • Take turns speaking
  • Admit when you are wrong
  • Check in daily, even briefly

Avoid

  • Interrupt or raise your voice
  • Bring up old issues as weapons
  • Give the silent treatment
  • Argue over text messages

Try This

  1. Name the issue in one sentence.
  2. Say how it affects you, without blame.
  3. Ask for their view and listen fully.
  4. Agree on one small change each.
"The biggest problem in communication is that we do not listen to understand. We listen to reply."
- Common saying

Common Challenges

Misunderstandings

Ask "What did you mean by that?" before assuming the worst.

Avoiding hard talks

Small issues ignored today become big ones later. Raise them gently and early.

Different styles

Some people talk things out, others need time first. Agree on how you will each handle conflict.
